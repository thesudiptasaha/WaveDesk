# Ideas to make WaveDesk more professional for a news / politics social-media agency (2026-10-30, ideas only, nothing built)

Owner asked: what else can be added, what makes it more professional, in line with his philosophy. Philosophy as Claude reads it from the project (to be confirmed by the owner): accountability (every action logged and credited), keep it simple and do one thing at a time, a small team where people wear many hats, quality gates before anything goes out, own your data and avoid risky outside dependencies, Bengali-first.

## Decision
- 2026-10-30: WaveDesk is for the agency's OWN channels only. No outside clients, so no client approval view, client reports or billing.

## A. Editorial integrity (most relevant for news and politics)
1. Sources and fact-check sign-off: a sources list on each script and a "fact-checked by" tick (a second pair of eyes) before Script Approved.
2. Corrections and takedown log: when something published is corrected or removed, record what, why, who, and the public correction text. Fits the existing "Published, later removed" status.
3. Sensitivity level on a content (normal / sensitive / legal risk) that adds an extra approver step.
4. Right-of-reply log for stories that name people or parties.

## B. Protection
5. Private / restricted contents: visible only to named people until published (important for political stories and embargoes). Needs the database security session first.
6. Source protection: no real names of confidential sources in normal fields; restricted notes.
7. Field safety check-in for reporters on risky political coverage (arrived / leaving / okay), with an emergency contact.

## C. Rights and legal
8. Rights register for footage, photos, music and archive clips: source, licence, expiry.
9. Guest consent: a record that a guest agreed to appear (links to the guest list already planned).

## D. Speed with control
10. Breaking-news fast lane: priority flag that skips the normal lead times but still needs a named sign-off, shown clearly on the Rundown.
11. On-duty roster / shifts, especially for the closed day (Friday) so someone is always responsible.
12. Event coverage packs for big days (budget day, elections, parliament sessions): a ready set of tasks and shoots.

## E. Planning and learning
13. Story ideas inbox before Draft: pitch -> accepted, with beats (politics, economy, ...).
14. Platform results per content (views, watch time) and a weekly report: output per person, on-time rate, corrections count. Targets / KPI much later.
15. Brand kit per channel: title / description / hashtag templates, thumbnail rules, end card, photocard generator (already on the later list).
16. Searchable archive: find what was said about a person or topic and when (accountability).
17. Subtitles / transcript workflow for Bengali videos.

## F. Running the team
18. Daily digest to the owner: what went out, what was missed, what is overdue.
19. Onboarding checklist for new hires and a role hand-over view (as dedicated people replace multi-hat staff).

## Claude's suggested first three (simple, high value, do not depend on the tasks system)
Corrections log, fact-check tick with sources, private / restricted contents (the last one after the database security session).
